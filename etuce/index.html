    How to try it

    1. 01

      Pick a learning goal

      Tap any box in the tree. You see what it means, and what it builds on.

    2. 02

      Copy the instruction

      The page writes an instruction for that goal, from the teacher's text and the shared teaching method. No AI is involved in writing it.

    3. 03

      Paste it into any AI chat

      ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, or whatever your school already has. The AI then practises that one goal with you, in your language.

    These trees are first drafts. An AI broke each national curriculum down with our open decomposition prompt, and no teacher in your country has checked them yet. That is the point of the method: a teacher reads the tree, fixes what is wrong, and shares it with colleagues.

    This website is translated by AI as well. A method like this should not stop at a language border.
