The prompts

Everything the AI is told

These are the instructions themselves, read straight from the method repository — not a summary of them. Each one says where it is used; open it to read it in full.

The instructions are written in English and say what the model should do, never in which language or subject. Three other layers fill the holes they leave open: the language layer, the subject family, and the tree's own file. Sections that come from a layer rather than from the instruction itself are marked.

The language layer and the subject family each have a card of their own further down, with a picker in it. Both choices also decide which marked sections stand inside the instructions above them.
