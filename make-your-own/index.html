For teachers

Make your own

A skill tree is one spreadsheet. Write it, drop it in below, and you get back a single HTML file with the whole tree inside it — no account, no installation, no cost.

Your file is not uploaded anywhere. It is opened in your own browser, and the HTML file is built there too — this page has no server to send it to.

Three ways in

Start wherever you are

  1. 01

    You have nothing yet

    Copy the instruction below into whichever AI chat you already use. It asks about your subject, your level and your curriculum, takes you through the decomposition, and hands you a spreadsheet at the end. Nothing to install.

    You are going to live with this
